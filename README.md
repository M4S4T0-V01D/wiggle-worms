# Wiggle Worms 🪱

An underground worm battle in the browser. Wiggle through the dirt, gobble snacks to grow, and make other worms bonk into you.

## How to play

- **Mouse** to steer. **Hold click** or **Space** to zoom (costs a little length).
- If your head bonks into another worm, you're out. Get them to bonk into *you*.
- Grab **power-up bubbles**: ⚡ Zoom, 🧲 Magnet, 👻 Ghost, 🍄 Mega.
- Collect 💎 **gems** to spend in the **Worm Shop** on patterns, hats, faces, tails, trails and titles.

## Worlds

| World | Unlocks | What's there |
|---|---|---|
| 🌱 Backyard Garden | Lv 1 | Soft dirt, lots of snacks |
| 🪨 Rocky Ground | Lv 3 | Rolling rocks to dodge |
| 🌋 Lava Pit | Lv 5 | A lava pool that pulls you in, plus heat waves |
| 🌀 Swirly Sinkhole | Lv 8 | Everything swirls toward the drain |
| 💎 Crystal Cave | Lv 12 | WORMZILLA, King of the Dirt |

Harder worlds pay more gems. Quests refresh as you finish them, and progress is saved in your browser.

## Running it

No build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
```
